The web console now runs in three places: `krishiv.ui()` (Python) and `krishiv::ui(&session)` (Rust) serve the
console of your own session on `127.0.0.1` behind a token; `krishiv server` serves every session, with the
settings page for administrators only, History filters by client and tag, and history kept across restarts when
`krishiv.history.path` is set; `krishiv ui --profiles <dir>` opens stored profiles offline. Released binaries
and wheels carry the console.
The console fits its screen from a 360 px phone to an ultrawide monitor: tables fit or scroll inside their own box,
touch targets are at least 44 px, and Run and Cancel of the SQL editor stay in view under a phone's keyboard.
