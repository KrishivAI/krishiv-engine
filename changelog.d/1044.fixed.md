With no `krishiv.memory.limit` set, a small container no longer gets its process killed by the kernel during a large join or group-by: the default memory limit is now lowered so that the pool, plus what the process may hold beyond it (an eighth of the pool and 64 MiB), leaves at least 384 MiB of the container for the page cache and spill writes (but never takes more than half of it). A 1 GiB container now gets a 512 MiB pool (was 819 MiB), a 2 GiB container about 1.39 GiB (was 1.6 GiB) and a 4 GiB container about 3.17 GiB (was 3.2 GiB); from about 4.4 GiB up the default is 80% as before.
