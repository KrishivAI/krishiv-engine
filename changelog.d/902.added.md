Declared Iceberg REST catalogs can be read: `SELECT` over `cat.ns.t` opens the table the catalog's `loadTable` returned through the Iceberg connector (`storage = allowed-prefixes`; `vended` and distributed reads are refused), `SHOW NAMESPACES [IN cat]`, `SHOW TABLES IN cat.ns` and the `spark.catalog` listings reach the catalog with the caller's token, `loadTable` answers are cached 30 s per table and identity, and `REFRESH TABLE cat.ns.t` (and `spark_catalog.ns.t`) drops them.
