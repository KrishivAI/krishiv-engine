A syntax error after an operator followed by a keyword now names the token and position Spark 4.2.0 names: `SELECT (1 +` then `FROM range(1)` on the next line reads `FROM` as a column and `range` as its alias, so the error is at the `(` (line 2, pos 10), not at `FROM`. A call whose first argument is an expression, a qualified name or a field no longer adds a `missing ')'` hint that Spark does not give.
