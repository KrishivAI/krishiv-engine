The default Spark profile is 4.3: `version()`, Spark Connect's `spark_version` and Python's `spark.version` answer 4.3.0. Under 4.3, `PIVOT` with duplicate values gives each output the slot of its own value (Spark 4.3.0; 4.2 raises `INVALID_ARRAY_INDEX`); duplicates with several aggregates and ANSI off stay refused until a 4.3 golden exists.
