`spark.sparkContext`, `df.rdd`, `krishiv.SparkConf`, `krishiv.pandas`, `krishiv.ml`, `SparkSession.builder.enableHiveSupport()` and the JDBC reader and writer now raise `KRISHIV_UNSUPPORTED_FEATURE` and name the DataFrame way, instead of a bare `AttributeError` or `ModuleNotFoundError` (the attributes are also `AttributeError`, the modules `ImportError`). Cluster settings (`master("yarn")`, `spark.executor.*`, `spark.submit.*`, `spark.driver.*`) warn that they have no effect. `krishiv.sql.pandas.utils` has the `require_minimum_*` checks.
