A hash join whose build side is estimated from a guessed filter (`LIKE`, `HAVING`) keeps a Bloom runtime filter when the build grows past its planned size or spills, so the probe-side scan still drops non-matching rows: TPC-H q09 at scale factor 1000 runs 15% faster (cold, 20 GB memory limit, 12 threads; probe spill 11.2 GB to 2.5 GB).
