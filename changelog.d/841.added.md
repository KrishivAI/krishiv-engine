The admin API `krishiv.admin.v1` can list and end sessions and queries (`ListSessions`, `KillSession`, `ListQueries`, `CancelQuery`, `CancelQueriesByTag`) over REST and gRPC. Your own work needs no extra role; other principals' work needs `Monitor` or `Operate`. Mutating calls take a `request_id` or `Idempotency-Key` and write one action record each.
