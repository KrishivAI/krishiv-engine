`spark.catalog` in Python now returns what PySpark 4.2 returns: lists of `Database`, `Table`, `Column`, `Function` and `CatalogMetadata` records (PY-026), not DataFrames. `spark_catalog` is now the only catalog: `setCurrentCatalog("system")` and `USE CATALOG system` fail with `CATALOG_NOT_FOUND`, and `listCatalogs()` returns just `spark_catalog` (`USE system` still switches the database).
