A `GROUP BY` that lists both a column and expressions over it (such as ClickBench q36, `GROUP BY ClientIP, ClientIP - 1, ...`) now groups by the column alone and computes the expressions once per group, with the same rows and errors (`krishiv.optimizer.derivedKeysAbove`, on by default). ClickBench q36 on a 12-thread server: 1.05 s to 0.86 s (0.82x, paired ABBA, median of 15 warm runs).
