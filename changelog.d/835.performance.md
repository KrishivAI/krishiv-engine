With `krishiv.optimizer.derivedKeysAbove=true`, a `GROUP BY` that lists both a column and expressions over it (such as ClickBench q36, `GROUP BY ClientIP, ClientIP - 1, ...`) groups by the column alone and computes the expressions once per group, with the same rows and errors. One indicative ClickBench q36 run on a 12-thread server: 4.26 s to 2.86 s.
