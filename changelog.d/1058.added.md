Delta tables with deletion vectors enabled run `DELETE`, `UPDATE` and `MERGE` as merge-on-read: the changed rows are marked in a deletion vector instead of rewriting their files, as delta-spark does (`spark.databricks.delta.{delete,update,merge}.deletionVectors.persistent`). Delta writes take delta-spark's write options: `mergeSchema`, `userMetadata` (also from `spark.databricks.delta.commitInfo.userMetadata`, on DML and an empty `CREATE TABLE` too), with `DELTA_ILLEGAL_OPTION` and `DELTA_INVALID_IDEMPOTENT_WRITES_OPTIONS` for bad values; `saveAsTable` append and `writeTo(..).append()` pass them.
