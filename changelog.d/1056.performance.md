A small scan now waits for a runtime filter whose build reads only its own, much smaller table
(`krishiv.runtimeFilter.smallTableWait`, on by default). Before, TPC-DS q02 and q59 could start that scan before the
filter was ready. Readers of a shared plan no longer queue on one lock: TPC-DS q14 no longer hits its slow mode, and
q67 runs at 0.80x. `krishiv.exec.dataflowScheduling` (off by default) starts each pipeline group as soon as its own
inputs have finished, instead of waiting for its whole wave.
