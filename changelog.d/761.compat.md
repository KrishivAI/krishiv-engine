Lambda parameters may be named by any SQL keyword, as in Spark: `array_sort(arr, (left, right) -> ...)` and lambdas over `from`, `case`, `not` or `interval` now parse. `CREATE FUNCTION f AS 'class'` now reports an unsupported feature instead of a syntax error.
`CREATE MATERIALIZED VIEW`, `CREATE STREAMING TABLE` and `CREATE FLOW` now parse and are refused with `UNSUPPORTED_FEATURE.CREATE_*_QUERY_EXECUTION`, as in Spark (SQL-20).
`LOAD DATA` now parses and fails as Spark does on a datasource table; `SELECT ?` and `SELECT :p` without arguments raise `UNBOUND_SQL_PARAMETER`, and mixing both `INVALID_QUERY_MIXED_QUERY_PARAMETERS` (SQL-17).
`INSERT OVERWRITE [LOCAL] DIRECTORY` now parses, with Spark's path rules, and is refused as unsupported until the write path lands (RS-10).
Spark's unnamed errors raise `ParseException` (`_LEGACY_ERROR_TEMP_0xxx`) or `AnalysisException` (`_LEGACY_ERROR_TEMP_1xxx`) in Python, so `lag` with a window frame raises `AnalysisException` (SQL-18).
