`SELECT ... FROM UNNEST(array, ...) [WITH ORDINALITY]` (Spark 4.3): one row per element, several arrays side by side padded with NULL, a 1-based `ordinality` column. DataFrame `zip` over Spark Connect puts the columns of two projections of one source side by side, or fails with `ZIP_PLANS_NOT_MERGEABLE`. `INSERT INTO t (cols) REPLACE WHERE cond` parses (Spark 4.3 grammar; under the 4.2 profile it is a `PARSE_SYNTAX_ERROR` at `REPLACE`) and is refused on a file table as Spark 4.3 does.
