Year-over-year queries over a `UNION ALL` of channels tagged by a constant now compute each channel's yearly
aggregates once for both years: a union drops the inputs a filter proves empty, as Spark's `PropagateEmptyRelation`
does, and the years then share one computation. TPC-DS q04, q11 and q74 use 0.86x, 0.90x and 0.92x the instructions
at scale factor 10 (server).
