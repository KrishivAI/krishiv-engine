`filter` takes the element index (`(x, i) -> ...`), `reverse` works on arrays, `sequence` works over dates and timestamps with year-month, day-time and calendar steps, `to_char` and `to_varchar` accept binary values (`hex`, `base64`, `utf-8`), and `schema_of_xml` honours `excludeAttribute`, `ignoreNamespace` and `prefersDecimal` and merges repeated elements the way Spark does (SQL-4, SQL-5, SQL-6).
