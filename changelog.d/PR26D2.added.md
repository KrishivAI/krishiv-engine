The Spark Connect translator reads `mapInPandas`/`mapInArrow`, `groupBy().applyInPandas`/`applyInArrow` and `cogroup().applyInPandas`/`applyInArrow` plans into the engine's Python UDF relations (running them over a Connect server waits for the worker pool's relation path). The analyzer resolves these relations, so a grouped or cogrouped map over a nondeterministic UDF fails at `df.schema` and `explain` with Spark's `INVALID_NON_DETERMINISTIC_EXPRESSIONS`, not only when it runs.
