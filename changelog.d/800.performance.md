A join whose probe side is estimated at least 20 times its build now keeps a runtime Bloom filter of up to 20 million keys (32 MiB) instead of falling back to a min/max range, and probes large filters with prefetching (`krishiv.runtimeFilter.bloom.atScale`, on by default). With `NOT EXISTS` and `EXISTS` subqueries over the same table, the more selective one now runs first (`krishiv.optimizer.semiAntiJoinOrder`). TPC-H SF100 CPU cycles: q04 -32%, q21 -23%, q05 -22%, q03 -15%, q09 -14% (12-core server); all 22 queries -2.1% wall on a laptop.
The final phase of a large hash aggregate allocates each partition's rows and aggregate states once, sized from its group estimate, instead of growing them by doubling: TPC-H q18 at SF100 uses 8-13% less CPU time and peaks 6-8% lower in memory (12-core server, two ABBA x5 runs). A task the memory pool cannot grant that up front merges as before. `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` reports `row_final_presized`.
