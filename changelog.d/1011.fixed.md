With `spark.sql.join.asofJoin.enabled` off, `FROM t ASOF JOIN q MATCH_CONDITION (...)` with no alias on `t` now raises `UNSUPPORTED_FEATURE.ASOF_JOIN`, as Spark 4.3 does, instead of a syntax error. Where Spark reads `ASOF` as an alias (`t ASOF JOIN q ON ...`, or `MATCH_CONDITION (a)` as the right side's alias with a column list), Krishiv does too; a Spark 4.2 session keeps 4.2's reading.
