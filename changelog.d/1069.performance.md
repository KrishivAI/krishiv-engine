Regex functions with a constant pattern (`regexp_replace`, `regexp_extract`, `RLIKE`) evaluate each distinct value
of a batch once, keep compiled patterns per thread, answer `RLIKE` without capture groups, and stop walking the
group-free tail of a pattern with the capture engine: ClickBench q29 takes 0.62x the time after the first two changes
and a further 0.67x to 0.72x after the third, and `RLIKE` over a column of repeated URLs 0.19x.
