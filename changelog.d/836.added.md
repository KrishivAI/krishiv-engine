Startup-declared catalogs are built and shared per runtime: a catalog limited to `tenants` is invisible to other principals (`SHOW CATALOGS`, `spark.catalog.listCatalogs`, `USE`), `system.catalogs` lists the visible ones without URIs or credentials, `krishiv.catalog.default` sets where a session starts, and a server refuses changes to catalog declaration keys at runtime. Iceberg REST catalogs connect through the egress policy; reading their tables comes in a later release.
