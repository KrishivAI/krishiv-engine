Under the Spark 4.3 profile a `TIME` casts to and from `TIMESTAMP` and `TIMESTAMP_NTZ` as Spark 4.3 does: a timestamp gives its time of day (in the session time zone for a `TIMESTAMP`), and a time takes today's date in the session time zone. These casts were `KRISHIV_UNSUPPORTED_FEATURE` before. `min` and `max` now take a `TIME` argument as well; before, they raised `KRISHIV_UNSUPPORTED_FEATURE`. The `TIME` functions (`hour`/`minute`/`second` and `extract` of a `TIME`, `time_trunc`, `time_diff`, `make_time`, `to_time`, `time_from_*`, `time_to_*`) report a nullable result, as Spark does.
