`krishiv server` hardens JWT logins (a lifetime cap, a required `exp`, the token id in the audit record) and adds service principals with trusted impersonation: a service such as a portal can act for an end user through act-as headers (over mTLS or loopback) or a signed act-as assertion. The end user's role is capped at the service's, `expires` ends a principal's rights for every login method, tokens carrying `act` are refused as logins, and act-as never crosses tenants. Every refusal is audited and counted; `system.sessions` and `system.queries` show who acted for whom (`delegated_by`).
