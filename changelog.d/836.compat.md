In server mode, `SET` and `RESET` of catalog declaration keys (`krishiv.catalog.<name>.*`, `spark.sql.catalog.<name>`, `spark.sql.catalog.<name>.*`) fail with `CANNOT_MODIFY_STATIC_CONFIG` for every role, where Spark accepts them at runtime; catalogs are declared at startup only. `SHOW CATALOGS` and `spark.catalog.listCatalogs` list every declared catalog the caller may see, including ones no query has used yet, while Spark lists only catalogs already loaded.
